Built with an enterprise-grade security mindset

We design Kharch Log the way serious finance apps should work: least privilege, clear consent, and no “upload your whole life to our cloud ledger.” That means:

  • Your Google Sheet is the source of truth — expenses sync to a sheet in your Drive when you enable Sheets sync.
  • Limited Google scopes — we ask for the Drive/Sheets access needed for the app folder/file you use, not blanket access to your entire Drive.
  • SMS stays on-device for capture — if you grant SMS permission, bank/UPI messages are read on your phone to draft expenses. We do not run an ad network on your SMS.
  • Review before it sticks — unclear messages land in a needs-review inbox so you stay in control.
  • HTTPS license checks — paid unlock is verified against our license API; payment cards are handled by Razorpay (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Sign in with Google — no separate password for us to lose.

No product is “unhackable,” but we refuse the common pattern of storing every rupee on a random third-party database you cannot export. Read the full story in our Privacy Policy.
