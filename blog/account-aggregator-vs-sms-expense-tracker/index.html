Do you need Account Aggregator for daily expense tracking?

2026-09-14

Account Aggregator is powerful for full net-worth views. For daily UPI kharch, bank SMS + Sheets is usually enough — with a clearer privacy trade-off.

Serious India finance writing now mentions Account Aggregator (AA) — and it should, for “see every bank in one place.” Daily expense tracking is a different job.

When AA makes sense

  • You want multi-bank balances, loans, and investments in one consent-based view
  • You are fine granting (and later revoking) financial-data consent

When SMS + Sheet is enough

  • Goal is “where did this week’s UPI go?”
  • You already get RBI-style debit SMS
  • You want the history in a file you can export forever
