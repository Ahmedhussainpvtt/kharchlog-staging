Daily expense tracker habits that actually stick

2026-08-25

Government guides like MoneySmart stress one idea: track spending before you optimize it. A daily expense tracker only works if capture is fast.

Three habits

  1. Same-day logging — use voice or SMS capture so you are not reconstructing the week on Sunday
  2. Categories over perfection — food, travel, bills beat 40 micro-tags you never use
  3. Weekly 10-minute review — open Today/reports or your Sheet and spot leaks
