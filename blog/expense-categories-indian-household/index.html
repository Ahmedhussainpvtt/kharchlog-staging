12 expense categories that work for Indian households

2026-09-11

Skip 40-tag chaos. A practical 12-category list for UPI + cash tracking in India — groceries, EMIs, transfers, and subscriptions — so weekly reviews stay usable.

Finance blogs in 2026 keep recommending the same idea: fewer categories, clearer decisions. Forty tags feel productive for a week, then nobody tags anything.

A defensible default list

  1. Groceries
  2. Food & dining
  3. Transport / fuel
  4. Utilities & rent
  5. Healthcare & insurance
  6. Education
  7. Shopping
  8. Subscriptions
  9. Entertainment & travel
  10. Personal transfers (family / friends)
  11. EMIs & loans
  12. Savings & investments

How to use them

  • Map payees once (Swiggy → Food). Next SMS inherits the rule.
  • Review one question monthly: “Which category surprised us?”
  • Keep “Misc” tiny — if it grows, split it.
