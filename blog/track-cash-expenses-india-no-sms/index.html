How to track cash expenses in India when there is no SMS

2026-09-10

Cash still skips bank SMS. A practical workflow for logging kirana, auto, and weekend cash spends — plus voice entry so your Google Sheet stays honest.

Competitors keep writing about UPI and SMS — and they should. But every honest India checklist also says: cash still moves, and cash sends no alert.

Why cash breaks “auto” trackers

  • Kirana, vegetables, autorickshaw, tips — often cash or “pay later”
  • No bank SMS means SMS-only apps silently under-report
  • Month-end looks fine until the wallet is empty

A 60-second cash habit

  1. Log cash the same day (not “tomorrow”).
  2. Use a short category: Food, Transport, Household, Misc.
  3. Prefer voice entry when you are walking — typing kills the habit.

Where Kharch Log fits

SMS auto-capture covers UPI/card debits. Cash and “missed” spends go in manually — then everything lands in your Google Sheet. You own the ledger; the app is not a locked vault.
