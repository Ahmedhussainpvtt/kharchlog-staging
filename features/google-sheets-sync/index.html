Common questions

Can I use an existing spreadsheet?

Kharch Log creates a dedicated sheet by default; you can copy rows into any workbook.

Does Kharch Log read my other Drive files?

No — only files it created under the limited scope.

What if I delete the app?

Your Google Sheet remains with every synced row.

Can two people log to one Sheet?

Share the Sheet via Google Drive; each person can use Kharch Log on their phone.

Is internet required?

Sync needs connectivity; manual entry can queue until online depending on device state.

How is this different from Fast Budget web app?

Fast Budget hosts data on their platform; Kharch Log uses your Google Sheet as the database.
