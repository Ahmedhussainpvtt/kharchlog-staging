Expense entry that survives real life

Common questions

Do I have to use voice?

No. Manual entry is first-class. Voice is optional when typing is awkward.

Can I edit entries after saving?

Yes — from the transaction list in the app or by editing rows in Google Sheets.

Does voice work offline?

Speech recognition usually needs a connection; manual entry works offline and syncs when back online.

How is this different from FinArt?

FinArt automates heavily from SMS and notifications across Android/iOS. Kharch Log emphasises Sheet ownership and a review inbox for unclear SMS — you stay the approver.

How is this different from Fast Budget?

Fast Budget offers optional bank sync and premium online storage. Kharch Log is free, stores in your Sheet, and never asks for net-banking passwords.

What fields can I log?

Amount, category, payment mode, payee, notes, date, and income vs expense type.
