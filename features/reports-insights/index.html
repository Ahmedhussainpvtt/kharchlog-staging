Common questions

Do reports require SMS capture?

No — manual, voice, and approved SMS entries all appear.

Can I view reports on desktop?

Yes — open your synced Google Sheet in a browser.

How often do reports update?

As soon as entries sync to your Sheet.

Does Kharch Log set budgets automatically?

Not currently — use category totals to inform budgets you track in Sheets.

Can I filter by payee?

Transaction list supports search and filters for payee and category.

Is Today view the same as daily budget?

Today shows what you logged today — a snapshot, not a spending limit.
