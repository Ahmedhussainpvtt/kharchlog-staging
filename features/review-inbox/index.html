Quick answer: Unclear or low-confidence SMS parses wait in the needs-review inbox — you approve, reject, or edit before anything hits your Google Sheet.

Human-in-the-loop control for automated capture

Automation should reduce typing, not introduce phantom expenses. FinArt and enterprise tools like Kredmint use review workflows; Kharch Log brings a personal version — you are the approver for anything the SMS parser is unsure about.

How the review inbox works

  1. SMS arrivesKharch Log parses the message on-device and scores confidence — amount, merchant, debit vs credit.
  2. Unclear → inboxPromotional SMS, OTPs, balance enquiries, and ambiguous debits stay out of your Sheet until you decide.
  3. Review side by sideSee parsed amount, category hint, payee, and the original SMS snippet in one place.
  4. Approve, reject, or editAccept clean matches in one tap; reject junk; fix category or amount before sync.

Why approval beats silent automation

Fast Budget can auto-import bank transactions — but wrong categorisation still happens. With SMS parsing, formats vary by bank. A review step keeps your Sheet trustworthy without giving up automation entirely.

Common questions

Can I approve from transaction detail?

Yes — pending SMS items show approve and reject actions until you decide.

What happens to rejected SMS?

Dismissed — never written to your Google Sheet.

Do manual entries use the inbox?

No — only auto-captured SMS needing confirmation appear there.

How often should I check the inbox?

Once daily is enough for most users — or enable notifications if available.

Can I edit then approve?

Yes — change category, payee, or amount before syncing.

Does rejection delete the SMS from my phone?

No — it only prevents Kharch Log from logging that message as an expense.
