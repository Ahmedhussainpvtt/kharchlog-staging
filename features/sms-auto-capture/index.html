Automatic tracking without sharing bank passwords

FinArt describes Indian finance as UPI, cards, wallets, EMIs, and bank SMS — not one clean feed. Kharch Log is built for that reality: SMS permission is required so payment alerts become ledger rows while keeping bank credentials out of the workflow, similar to FinArt’s “no bank login” positioning.

How SMS auto-capture works

  1. Install & sign inLink your Google account so parsed expenses have somewhere durable to land — your Kharch Log Sheet in Drive.
  2. Grant SMS permissionRequired. Kharch Log needs SMS access to read bank and UPI payment alerts on your phone.
  3. Payment SMS arrivesDebit and credit alerts from banks and UPI apps are parsed on-device for amount, merchant hint, and date.
  4. Review or auto-saveHigh-confidence matches sync to Sheets. Unclear messages go to the review inbox — never silently invent spending.

Common questions

Is SMS permission required?

Yes. SMS permission is required so Kharch Log can auto-capture bank and UPI payment messages.

Does Kharch Log upload my SMS to a server?

No. Parsing happens on-device; matched rows sync to your Google Sheet.

Will every bank SMS parse perfectly?

No — formats vary. Unclear items wait in the review inbox for your decision.

Does this work on iPhone?

Kharch Log is Android-only. iOS restricts SMS access differently — use manual/voice entry.

Can I disable SMS capture later?

Yes — revoke SMS permission in Android settings and continue logging manually.

What about OTP or promo SMS?

Non-transaction messages should land in the review inbox or be ignored — not your expense Sheet.
