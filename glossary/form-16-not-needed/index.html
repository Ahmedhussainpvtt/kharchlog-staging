Form 16 not needed

Kharch Log is a personal daily expense tracker — not tax software. You do not need Form 16 to use it, and it does not generate Form 16 or file ITR for you.

In Kharch Log, this ties into daily logging, UPI/bank SMS capture, and a Google Sheet you own. See features or the FAQ.
