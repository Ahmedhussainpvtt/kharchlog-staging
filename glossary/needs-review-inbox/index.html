Needs-review inbox

A queue for SMS-detected transactions that are unclear or incomplete — approve, reject, or edit before they become permanent ledger rows.

In Kharch Log, this ties into daily logging, UPI/bank SMS capture, and a Google Sheet you own. See features or the FAQ.
